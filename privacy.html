1. Introduction

EvPay is a digital payments platform operated by EvMak Tanzania Limited (“EvMak”, “EvPay”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of the personal information entrusted to us by our customers, merchants, partners, website visitors, and users of our products and services.

As a licensed payment service provider, we recognize the importance of maintaining the trust of our users and complying with applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards governing data protection, financial services, and payment processing. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, protect, and process personal information when you access or use EvPay’s websites, applications, merchant portals, APIs, payment services, and any related products offered by EvMak.

By accessing or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • EvPay payment gateway services.
  • EvPay merchant and customer portals.
  • EvPay mobile and web applications.
  • APIs and developer services.
  • Payment collection and settlement services.
  • Customer support interactions.
  • Any website, platform, or service operated by EvMak Tanzania Limited that references this Privacy Policy.

3. Information We Collect

To provide our services safely and effectively, we may collect the following categories of information:

3.1 Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • National identification information
  • Passport information (where applicable)
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Physical address
  • Postal address

3.2 Business Information

For merchants and corporate customers:

  • Business name
  • Company registration documents
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  • VAT information
  • Beneficial ownership information
  • Business licenses and permits
  • Authorized representative information

3.3 Financial Information

  • Bank account details
  • Settlement account information
  • Mobile money account details
  • Payment card information (processed securely through approved partners)
  • Transaction history
  • Payment preferences

3.4 Technical and Usage Information

  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • Login activity
  • Session information
  • Platform usage statistics
  • Cookies and similar technologies

4. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only where necessary to operate our business, provide services, and comply with legal obligations. Your information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Process payments and settlements.
  • Verify customer and business identities.
  • Perform KYC and KYB procedures.
  • Prevent fraud, money laundering, and financial crime.
  • Monitor transaction activity.
  • Respond to customer inquiries and support requests.
  • Improve platform functionality and user experience.
  • Communicate important service updates.
  • Generate operational and analytical reports.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

EvPay processes personal information based on one or more of the following grounds:

  • Performance of a contract.
  • Compliance with legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Legitimate business interests.
  • Protection against fraud and financial crime.
  • User consent where required by law.

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

EvPay does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties. We may share information only when necessary with:

Financial Institutions

Banks, acquiring institutions, card schemes, payment processors, and mobile network operators involved in transaction processing and settlement.

Regulatory Authorities

Government agencies, law enforcement authorities, financial regulators, courts, and supervisory bodies where disclosure is required by law.

Service Providers

Technology vendors, cloud service providers, cybersecurity providers, and professional advisors supporting our operations.

Business Partners

Strategic partners that help deliver services to customers, subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

All third parties receiving information are required to maintain appropriate safeguards and use the information only for authorized purposes.

7. Fraud Prevention and Financial Crime Compliance

EvPay maintains a comprehensive risk management framework designed to protect customers, merchants, financial institutions, and the broader payment ecosystem. We may:

  • Verify identities and business ownership.
  • Monitor transactions for suspicious activity.
  • Conduct sanctions screening.
  • Perform fraud detection and risk assessments.
  • Investigate unusual transactions.
  • Restrict, suspend, or terminate accounts where fraud or misuse is suspected.
  • Report suspicious transactions to relevant authorities where required by law.

Failure to provide requested information may result in limited access to certain services.

8. Data Security

Protecting customer information is one of EvPay’s highest priorities. We employ appropriate technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards, including:

  • End-to-end encrypted communications.
  • SSL/TLS encryption.
  • Secure API authentication mechanisms.
  • Multi-layer access controls.
  • Firewalls and intrusion prevention systems.
  • Transaction monitoring tools.
  • Fraud detection systems.
  • Secure data storage and backup procedures.
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments.

Although we implement robust security measures, no system can guarantee absolute security. Users are encouraged to maintain the confidentiality of their login credentials and security information.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services.
  • Fulfill contractual obligations.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Enforce agreements.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, accounting, and tax requirements.

Certain financial and compliance records may be retained for extended periods as required by applicable laws and regulations.

10. International Data Transfers

In some circumstances, information may be processed or stored outside Tanzania through trusted technology partners and service providers. Where such transfers occur, EvPay implements reasonable safeguards to ensure personal information remains protected and is handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

11. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws and regulatory requirements, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where permitted.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Request information about how your data is used.

Requests may be subject to identity verification and legal limitations.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

EvPay uses cookies and related technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality.
  • Enhance user experience.
  • Maintain security.
  • Analyze platform performance.
  • Remember user preferences.

Users may manage cookie preferences through their browser settings.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

EvPay may amend this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, technology, products, or business practices. Updated versions will be published on our website and platforms. Continued use of our services following publication constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
